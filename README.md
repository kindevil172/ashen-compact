# Ashen Compact

A dark-fantasy idle roguelike. You lead a mercenary company through the Kingdom of Varn — a land torn by a succession war and the Ashen Choir. Battles are 1v1 and automatic. Start with one fighter and grow the company to three.

## Play

Open the game in any browser (phone or PC):

**https://kindevil172.github.io/ashen-compact/**

You can also download `index.html` and open it locally. It is a single self-contained file; no server is required.

## How to play

- After each fight, keep your active fighter or switch to another company member.
- The fighter who battled earns full XP; other company members earn half.
- Equip weapons, armor, and trinkets. Set techniques, spells, and gambit rules.
- There is no meta progression — each run starts fresh.
